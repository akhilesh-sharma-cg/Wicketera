Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information WICKETRA handles through its public website and administrator dashboard.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Information handled by WICKETRA

WICKETRA displays cricket match and tournament information. Depending on how the website is used, information entered into the administration dashboard may include:

  • Administrator account information: email address and account identifier used by Supabase Authentication to sign in and verify administrator access.
  • Team information: team name, short name and city.
  • Player information: player name, team association and playing role.
  • Tournament information: tournament name, description, venue, city, state, dates, format, overs and entry-fee value.
  • Match and scoring information: teams, match format, schedule, venue, innings, runs, wickets, player selections and optional ball-by-ball commentary.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into public match, team or player fields. The dashboard code reviewed for this policy does not provide a public visitor registration or contact-submission form.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate the website, display match and tournament details, manage teams and players, maintain scores and standings, and authenticate authorized administrators.

3. Service providers and data storage

WICKETRA uses Supabase for its database and administrator authentication. Information submitted through the website is transmitted to Supabase and processed under its services and policies. The website also loads the Supabase JavaScript library from a content delivery network.

WICKETRA includes Google AdSense advertising code on the public website. If advertisements are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertising, subject to Google's policies and the settings available to you. The presence of the code does not mean that ads are currently being displayed.

4. Cookies and browser storage

Supabase Authentication may use browser storage and related technologies to maintain an administrator's sign-in session. Third-party services, including advertising services when active, may also use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling necessary storage may affect admin sign-in or website functionality.

5. Publicly visible information

Match, team, player and tournament information may appear on the public WICKETRA website so visitors can follow competitions. Administrators should only add information they are authorized to publish and should inform players or teams as appropriate.

6. Data security and retention

We rely on the security features of the services used to operate WICKETRA and take reasonable care when managing access. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information is retained as needed to operate the website, maintain records and meet applicable requirements. The exact retention period may depend on the relevant Supabase database and account settings.

7. Your choices and requests

For questions or requests concerning information associated with WICKETRA, contact us at akhileshsharma.cgs@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request is genuine before acting on it. You can also manage browser cookies through your browser settings.

8. Children's privacy

WICKETRA is a general cricket information and tournament-management website. It is not designed to request sensitive personal information from children. Please do not submit sensitive personal details through team, player or commentary fields.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as WICKETRA's features or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email akhileshsharma.cgs@gmail.com.
