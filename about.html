About WICKETRA

The Pulse of Every Ball. A simple place to follow cricket matches and manage tournaments.

What is WICKETRA?

WICKETRA is a cricket-focused platform designed to make following and managing cricket matches and tournaments simpler. The website presents match information, live scores, upcoming matches, recent results and tournament standings.

What you can do

  • Follow available live match scores and ball-by-ball updates.
  • Explore upcoming matches and recent results.
  • View tournament information and points tables.
  • Authorized administrators can manage tournaments, teams, players, fixtures and scoring through the admin dashboard.

Our aim

Our aim is to provide cricket fans, teams and tournament organizers with a straightforward place to follow the game and manage cricket competitions.
